## Application and applicability

PesoMatch is currently a local academic Philippine employment-facilitation platform: profiling, vacancies, advisory matching/referrals, training, PESO assistance, placement and outcome monitoring. React/Vite communicates with Laravel/Sanctum and MySQL; installed web/native clients share that service. Source-linked PhilJobNet and TESDA records are catalog snapshots, not authenticated integrations or proof of partnership. There is no checkout, subscription, payment processor, analytics/advertising SDK or enabled marketing-mail pipeline in the active application. Email recovery/verification remains disabled and is described accurately in the account UI.

Processing Philippine residents' personal information requires assessment under [RA 10173](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act-/). Names/contact details, profiling and service records are personal information; age/birth-date, education and some qualification details require a sensitive-information assessment. An academic project must not assume that naming PESO gives it a public-authority processing mandate. The actual operator must document purpose-specific lawful criteria, recipients and safeguards under the [DPA implementing rules](https://privacy.gov.ph/implementing-rules%20regulations-data-privacy-act-2012/).

The registration checkbox now records terms acceptance and notice acknowledgement, not blanket consent. The NPC distinguishes privacy notices from a lawful consent-based processing arrangement; review each purpose and avoid bundling unrelated uses into required terms. See the [NPC consent guidelines](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/NPC-Circular-No.-2023-04_Guidelines-on-Consent_07Nov2023.pdf).

[RA 11967](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/96902) and its [DTI implementing rules](https://ecommerce.dti.gov.ph/implementing-rules-and-regulations/) concern covered internet commercial transactions. This free employment workflow has no payment/checkout; applicability is conditional on the operator and any commercial training/service arrangement. Displaying provider fees or links is not enough to assert either complete exemption or full marketplace compliance. Counsel should assess those boundaries. Consumer representations and any paid-service arrangement also warrant review under [RA 7394](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/3302); no fictional refund rights or blanket waiver was introduced.

[WCAG 2.2 AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) is used as the technical accessibility target. Automated audits and tested interactions cover specific states; they do not substitute for screen-reader, cognitive-accessibility, real-device and complete manual conformance testing, or establish which statutory procurement/accessibility rules apply to the eventual operator.

No automatic processing restriction, third-party erasure API, backup purge or legal-hold engine was invented behind the new request form. Those require actual organizational decisions and infrastructure integration. The operator should implement the approved process and document evidence of action under the NPC's [data subject rights](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-subject-rights/) and [erasure/blocking guidance](https://privacy.gov.ph/right-to-erasure-or-blocking/), rather than promise unconditional immediate deletion.

## Required approvals before public launch

1. Identify the real operator/PESO authority and publish verified support/privacy contacts, including a route for people unable to sign in.
2. Approve purpose-specific lawful bases and recipient arrangements, sensitive/minor-data safeguards, a retention schedule including logs/backups, privacy impact assessment, access/incident procedures and relevant NPC registration/DPO obligations.
3. Approve and operationalize rights-request handling, reviewed deletion/restriction, legal holds, backup expiry/restore behavior and third-party/device-copy limitations. Do not equate a database status update with completed erasure.
4. Resolve unknown photo/poster/catalog reuse and identity/trademark rights; approve or replace assets before distribution, preserving required licenses.
5. Verify public hosting, HTTPS/security settings, recovery/support, processor locations/agreements and all production forms/communications. Conduct qualified legal review and a complete manual accessibility review with representative users.

Refunds, hidden platform charges, marketing unsubscribe and advertising-cookie controls are not fabricated for absent features. They must be revisited if the application begins offering those features or the commercial context changes.
