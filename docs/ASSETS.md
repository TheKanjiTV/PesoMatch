# AI-Powered Employment Facilitation Platform for Public Employment Service Office (PESO) — Local Assets

## Typography and identity

Public Sans and Lora are self-hosted in public/fonts/. Their SIL Open Font License files are included. Sources: [Public Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/publicsans) and [Lora](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/lora). The new PESO pathway mark is authored SVG; interface icons use the installed lucide-react library.

The active design uses typography, rules, restrained sage surfaces and task records. It does not reproduce supplied social-network or commercial job-board branding.

## User-supplied PESO posters

Existing poster and workplace images are preserved on disk. They are no longer the organizing structure of the public/service workspace and are not presented as live employment announcements.
