The Help page is available without sign-in, states the source-check date, provides accessible email/phone/source links, and makes no third-party request until the user chooses an external action. Email links do not prefill a résumé, account identifier or message. Source links open with `noopener noreferrer` and announce a new tab. Platform-specific privacy requests remain in authenticated Account Settings, including for incomplete profiles.

## Operator identity remains unverified

Public directories establish an agency's contact channels, not who controls this application's personal information. No appointment, adoption agreement, hosting responsibility, verified organizational address or DPO designation tying a specific office to PesoMatch was provided or discovered in repository evidence. Privacy/terms notices therefore retain explicit draft placeholders for the actual operator, address and DPO/support channels. Legal audit item 16 remains PARTIAL; a government's public address must not be substituted as the platform's business address.

## Privacy and accessibility basis for the follow-up

The [NPC Guidelines on Consent](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/NPC-Circular-No.-2023-04_Guidelines-on-Consent_07Nov2023.pdf) distinguish a privacy notice from consent and call for transparency when processing personal data. Point-of-collection explanations were added to profiling, résumé, referral/enrollment and service forms; these do not by themselves establish a lawful basis or approved guardian procedure.

The [W3C Focus Not Obscured guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum) informed keyboard checks around the mobile bottom bar and inline editors. Tests cover specific states and cannot establish complete WCAG conformance.
