# AI-Powered Employment Facilitation Platform for Public Employment Service Office (PESO) — PESO Responsibilities

## Source coordination for the requested Bansud workspace

PESO reviews local requests created from official-source listings. An external referral requires recorded employer availability/application verification. A source-training request requires a separately verified local intake before confirmation. PESO coordinates; employers decide hiring and providers supply actual admission, schedule, fees, places and completion evidence. The application does not automatically submit profiles to agencies or issue TESDA credentials. See [current workflow](WORKSPACE-UPDATE-2026-11-07.md).

## Statutory service mapping

The platform supports officer workflows described in [RA 10691, Section 2 amending Section 5 of RA 8759](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2015/ra_10691_2015.html). The following mapping is a concise implementation interpretation, not a claim of complete legal compliance.

| PESO function | Platform support |
| --- | --- |
| Employment information and labor-market exchange | Published vacancies; Labor Market Information |
| Testing/evaluation for selection, training, counseling | Employability assessment requests and officer notes |
| Livelihood and self-employment referrals | Livelihood service case and referral destination |
| Employability enhancement | Skills recommendations and Training Enrollment |
| Occupational counseling and career guidance | Career counseling requests and program events |
| Pre-employment orientation | Orientation cases and schedules |
| Returning-worker reintegration | Reintegration assistance cases |
| Required reporting | Vacancy CSV, outcome monitoring, auditable records |
| Other employment facilitation functions | Job fairs, special-group and program coordination |

## Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Jobseeker | Register; maintain truthful profile; request referrals/enrollment; attend services; report employment status |
| PESO officer | Review profiles/catalog; facilitate referrals and interviews; coordinate providers/programs; verify placement and outcomes |
| Administrator | PESO operations; provision/revoke roles on existing accounts; inspect audits |
| Employer | Submit own vacancy data for review; coordinate referred candidates; record interview/offer/selection updates |
| Training provider | Submit own programs for review; check eligibility/capacity; confirm enrollment; record progress/completion |

Training coordination follows a request/confirmation distinction consistent with [TESDA's published enrollment guidance](https://knowledgebase-bsrs.tesda.gov.ph/article/how-to-enroll-to-a-program). Program-specific requirements are set by the actual provider.

## Operational limits

PESO staff still conduct counseling, administer assessments, check employer legitimacy, verify evidence, and coordinate livelihood/reintegration/program referrals outside the software. The platform records those services and their schedules/results. It does not grant government benefits, perform official competency assessment, issue National Certificates, or enroll people into TESDA systems. Formal LGU reporting templates and privacy policy require the pilot office's input.
